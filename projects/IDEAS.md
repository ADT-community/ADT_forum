# Project Ideas

A starting list. Propose new ideas with the **Project proposal** issue template.

| Project | Problem | How it could earn | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Creator platform** (short video) | African creators are often shut out of big platforms' creator payouts | Ads, tips, brand deals, premium features; pays creators via mobile money | Hard |
| **Mobile money toolkit** | Developers struggle to integrate payments across many countries | Hosted API, paid support | Medium |
| **African language AI** | Few AI tools work well in African languages | API access, licensing to companies and governments | Medium–Hard |
| **Offline-first learning app** | Data costs and poor connectivity block learning | School and NGO contracts, sponsorship | Medium |
| **Farm advisory bot** (SMS/WhatsApp) | Smallholder farmers lack timely advice on weather, prices, pests | Subscriptions, agri-business partnerships | Medium |
| **Solar mini-grid monitor** (hardware) | Off-grid solar systems fail without anyone noticing | Device sales, monitoring subscription | Hard |
| **Low-cost health devices** (hardware) | Clinics lack affordable diagnostic tools | Device sales, grants | Hard |

## Suggested first projects

Start with 2–3 projects that can earn quickly and need little infrastructure, such as the mobile money toolkit or the farm advisory bot. These build trust in the payout model. Big projects like the creator platform become flagships once the community and the funding are in place.

## Creator platform: notes

This is the most ambitious idea. Things to plan for:

- **Costs:** video storage and streaming are expensive, so you need a CDN strategy early.
- **Moderation:** needs tools and people, and must follow each country's laws.
- **Creator payouts:** mobile money (M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money) through providers like Flutterwave or Paystack.
- **Growth:** pick one or two countries and a niche, such as music or comedy, before going continent-wide.
